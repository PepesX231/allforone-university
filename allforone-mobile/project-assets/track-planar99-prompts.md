## Game

A sophisticated hand-designed TWO-DIMENSIONAL SCREEN-PRINTED PAPER COLLAGE, isolated on transparent background. Every part is a flat piece of cut paper seen straight-on. Use flat solid ink only, angular rectangles, trapezoids and triangles, irregular deliberate hand-cut edges, thick dark-navy ink outline. Depth is created ONLY by slightly offset duplicated silhouettes and separate flat paper pieces overlapping, like a pop-up graphic poster whose pieces remain parallel to the page. DO NOT show any side wall, bevel, curved surface, raised volume, bulging shape or facet shading on the objects. NO 3D perspective, NO isometric model, NO low-poly rendering, NO glossy highlights, NO gradients, NO soft shadows. ONE hard navy offset shadow behind a few whole paper layers, not shading the surface of objects. It must look like a beautifully composed printed graphic illustration, not a technical diagram or simple computer icon: expressive diagonal silhouette, varied shape scale, controlled dense and empty areas, tiny halftone accents in a couple of navy shapes. Palette flat COBALT BLUE, vivid YELLOW, warm CREAM, NAVY and one small coral-red accent. Layered cardboard poster effect, bold playful aggressive Japanese pop-comic art. The backing silhouette is angular and cream, NOT a rounded sticker rim. Entire complete composition with generous transparent margins, landscape 4:3. No text, lettering, labels, logos, stars, lightning, mascots, circular decorations, floating confetti or background panel. Subject: GAME: a flat cream controller face cut as a broad angular polygon with TWO triangular grips, a flat yellow cross D-pad, two flat navy hexagon patches with zero thickness, four flat square yellow buttons. No cylindrical thumbsticks, no round domed buttons. Behind it an overlapping front-facing rectangular game-screen paper cutout tilted by about eight degrees. Inside the screen, a playful simple game collage made from triangular blue mountains, cream polygon clouds, green zigzag platform strips and a tiny flat yellow square player. Only a short dark offset paper shadow suggests distance between screen layer and controller layer. No side faces of controller or screen.
